How it works

  1. You get one small square of a confocal image at a time.
  2. Click once on every nucleus. A dot appears so you cannot lose your place or count it twice.
  3. Click a dot again to remove it. u or ⌘Z undoes the last one.
  4. Scroll to zoom, drag to move around. The sliders on the right brighten the image — use them freely, the app records where you set them.
  5. Only click inside the dashed box. A nucleus belongs to the square its centre falls in, so a cell half outside is counted by whoever gets the neighbouring square.
  6. If a square is empty, press Nothing here — that is a real answer, not a skip.
  7. Your work saves as you go. You can close the tab and come back to the same link.
