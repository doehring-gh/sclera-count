**Subject:** Re: — both done, and here is what it looks like

Dear Torsten,

Both of your points are now in the tool rather than on a list, so here is what
they look like.

**The greyscale nuclei channel is now the default.** It was always there — you
simply had no way to know, because the images I sent you were the merged view.
Counting now starts on greyscale Hoechst, and the merged view is one keypress
away for orientation. The first attachment shows the same square in all three
views with the same marks: find the nuclei on panel 1, flip to panel 2 to judge
each one, and the marks stay exactly where you put them.

Those marks are on real detections in that tile, not positions I placed to make
the picture look tidy.

**The markers now differ by shape as well as colour** — circle for live, square
for dead, triangle for unsure (second attachment). You were right that this is
more than an aesthetic problem, and colour-only coding was a genuine mistake on
my part: it fails outright for a red/green-blind counter and is hard to read on a
busy greyscale field for everyone else. The shapes are also taught on the "how to
count" page now rather than left to be discovered, which is the same error I made
with the greyscale channel.

Thank you for both — they are better fixed now than after twenty people have used
it.

I have replied separately on the Z-correction, which I think may unblock the
depth part of the study altogether rather than just improve the images.

Best wishes,
Daniela
